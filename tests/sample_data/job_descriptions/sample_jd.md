# Sample Job Description for Testing

## Senior Software Engineer - Backend

**Location:** Remote  
**Experience:** 5+ years  
**Type:** Full-time

### About the Role

We are seeking an experienced Senior Software Engineer to join our growing engineering team. You will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining our core backend services that power our platform used by millions of users worldwide.

### **Required Skills**

- **Programming:** 5+ years of professional software development experience
- **Languages:** Strong proficiency in **Python** and **Java**
- **Cloud:** Hands-on experience with **AWS** cloud services (EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS)
- **Containers:** Experience with **Docker** and **Kubernetes** for containerization
- **APIs:** Proven track record building **REST APIs** and microservices
- **Databases:** Strong knowledge of **PostgreSQL** or **MySQL**
- **Version Control:** Proficiency with **Git** and GitHub

### **Preferred Skills**

- Experience with **microservices architecture**
- Knowledge of **CI/CD** pipelines (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI)
- Familiarity with **Agile/Scrum** methodology
- Experience with message queues (**Kafka**, **RabbitMQ**)
- **FastAPI** or **Django** framework experience
- Previous **team leadership** or mentorship experience

### **Responsibilities**

- Design and implement scalable backend services
- Lead technical discussions and architectural decisions
- Mentor junior engineers and conduct code reviews
- Collaborate with product and frontend teams
- Optimize application performance and reliability
- Participate in on-call rotation for production systems

### **Soft Skills**

- **Strong communication** and collaboration skills
- **Problem-solving** mindset and analytical thinking
- Ability to **mentor** and guide junior team members
- **Ownership** and accountability for deliverables
- Passion for learning new technologies
- Detail-oriented with focus on code quality

### **Nice to Have**

- Contributions to open-source projects
- Experience with Terraform for infrastructure as code
- Knowledge of caching strategies (Redis, Memcached)
- Understanding of distributed systems
- Machine learning or data pipeline experience

---

**How to Apply:**  
Submit your resume highlighting your Python, AWS, and containerization experience.
