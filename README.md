# 🚀 ATS Forge: Deep Intelligence Resume & JD Analyzer

ATS Forge is a high-accuracy, LLM-powered tool designed to bridge the gap between candidate resumes and specific job requirements. It focuses on "Deep JD Intelligence"—understanding not just the words in a job description, but the mission and goals of the company.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective
To provide students and job seekers with a "Peak Potential" analysis of their resumes. By exhaustively dissecting the Job Description, the system identifies mandatory, preferred, and implicit requirements to provide a semantic ATS score and actionable improvement suggestions.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: [Streamlit](https://streamlit.io/) (Python) - *Fast, functional, and focused on data.*
- **Parsing**: `pdfplumber` - *Layout-aware PDF extraction to handle complex resume formats.*
- **LLM Engine**: [Google Gemini 1.5 Pro](https://ai.google.dev/) - *Top-tier reasoning and massive context window for deep intelligence.*
- **Language**: Python 3.9+

---

## 🔥 Key Features

### 1. 🧠 Deep JD Intelligence
Before matching, the tool performs a multi-layered analysis of the JD:
- **Role Mission**: Why is the company hiring? (e.g., Scaling, Innovation, Maintenance).
- **Requirement Taxonomy**: Categorizing skills into *Mandatory*, *Preferred*, and *Implicit*.
- **Company DNA**: Extracting culture and work-style patterns.
- **ATS Blueprint**: Generating high-precision keyword tokens for scanners.

### 2. 📄 Layout-Aware Resume Digestion
- Handles multi-column resumes without jumbling text.
- Extracts projects, experience, and certifications as distinct entities.

### 3. ✅ STAR Method Validation
- Analyzes resume bullets to ensure they follow the **Situation-Task-Action-Result** framework.
- Flags "weak" bullets that only list tasks without impact.

### 4. 🔄 Optimization Workspace
- **Real-time Scoring**: See how your ATS score changes as you edit.
- **HR Persona Feedback**: Get tips from the perspective of an "Enterprise Recruiter" vs. a "Startup Founder".
- **Tailored Assets**: Generate JD-specific cover letters and "Copy-Paste" ready bullet points.
